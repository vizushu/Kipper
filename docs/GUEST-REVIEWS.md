# Selected historical guest reviews

Ten complete reviews were transcribed from four screenshots supplied by the property operator, who confirmed all ten were from Casa Pacifica's Airbnb listing and requested their names/photos/text on this website. Duplicate screenshot entries are included only once. An incomplete eleventh entry is excluded at the operator's request. This is a selected collection, not the full review history and not a basis for an aggregate rating.

Published fields are only the displayed first name, avatar, review month/year, individual stars and full review text. No profile location, account tenure, trip category, screenshot interface, guest contact detail or other screenshot metadata is included. Quotes retain original spelling, punctuation and references to the historical host. Mixed feedback is retained in full; these historical descriptions must not redefine current inventory/access/amenity promises. No website-verified-booking badge or structured aggregate rating is generated.

The ten avatar assets are mechanical pixel crops of the original supplied screenshots, without generative editing, resizing or face modification. Each was visually checked against its corresponding name/photo in the originals. PNG outputs were copied into fresh RGB images to omit EXIF/text metadata. Full screenshots are not shipped. Filenames use only the already-displayed first names.

Cards expose two initial reviews and an accessible native disclosure containing all remaining supplied reviews in screenshot order. Full text remains available without editorial truncation, including the longest mixed review. The source/context notice is visible before the cards.
